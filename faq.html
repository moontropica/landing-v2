Participation

Community members can support the ecosystem by:

  • Holding $CAH — Simply holding the token supports the ecosystem
  • Providing Liquidity — Add LP to the canonical pool to earn fees and strengthen the economy
  • Trading — Active trading volume sustains development and growth
  • Staking — Stake to earn rewards and gain governance rights
  • Governance Voting — Shape the direction of the project

To get involved with the community, send a message to CAHmander on Telegram for onboarding.

Moon Tropica offers two staking options with 5% of the total supply allocated for staking incentives:

Standard Staking — Stake CAH tokens to earn passive rewards and participate in governance. Ideal for holders who want consistent returns without providing liquidity.

Liquidity Staking — Deposit CAH/ETH LP tokens in exchange for premium rewards, a share of DEX transaction fees, enhanced governance voting power, and a higher APY than standard staking.

Item Mining — Staking also unlocks access to exclusive in-game items, cosmetics, and limited-edition skins that cannot be earned any other way.

→ Full staking details

Moon Tropica features a two-tier governance system based on staking type:

  1. Liquidity Stakers — Can create governance proposals related to gameplay features, economic adjustments, treasury allocation, and more.
  2. Standard Stakers — Receive voting rights on proposals. Voting power is weighted by stake size.

Areas of governance influence include: feature proposals, crafting and item economy rules, seasonal events, fee/tax adjustments, protocol upgrades, roadmap direction, treasury delegation, and staking reward configurations.

Bonding

05

You give the protocol some ETH and CAH together (a Uniswap LP position). In return, it gives you back more CAH than you put in — but you have to wait a year to receive it all.

Step 1: You give a sandwich made of half ETH and half CAH (a Uniswap LP position).

Step 2: You get a promise from the protocol — roughly the value of your sandwich, paid in CAH.

Step 3: You wait. Nothing for the first 30 days. Then a small slice unlocks every day for the next ~11 months.

The catch: Once you bond, your LP is gone — the protocol keeps it forever. Don't bond money you'll need back.

→ Full bonding docs

  1. Provide a full-range CAH/ETH position — Either create a fresh full-range LP from the Bond page, or bond an LP NFT you already hold. The position must span the full price range of the canonical pool.
  2. The protocol values your LP at 2× its ETH side — A balanced full-range position has equal value on both sides. ETH side × 2 = total LP value in ETH.
  3. A multiplier is applied — Vesting value = LP value × (1 + discountBps/10,000).
  4. ETH-denominated value is converted to CAH — Using the canonical pool's spot price, with a price floor to protect against manipulation.
  5. A vesting schedule is created — 30-day cliff, then 335 days of linear unlock (365 days total).
  6. The LP NFT lives on the protocol forever — It cannot be reclaimed. The multisig can collect swap fees but cannot rug your vesting schedule.

→ Full bonding docs

  • Days 0–30 (Cliff): Nothing claimable. Your schedule exists but every claim returns 0.
  • Days 30–365 (Linear unlock): totalAmount × elapsed / 335 unlocks per second. Claim any time, partially or in full.
  • Day 365+ (Fully vested): Claim the remainder whenever — the schedule deactivates after every wei is collected.

Claims show a gross / fee / net breakdown before signing. The claim fee is currently 0.0% and hard-capped at 10%.

Full-range positions have a balanced, predictable two-sided value — ETH side × 2 = total ETH value, regardless of price moves. Concentrated positions can have nearly all of one side and almost none of the other, which makes valuation either unfair to the bonder or exploitable.

The canonical pool is the native-ETH/CAH pool with 3.14% (π%) fee, 628 (2π × 100) tick spacing, and no hook. It serves as the price oracle for bonds and is the destination for new bond positions. Only positions in this pool are accepted for bonding.

No. Bonding is one-way. The LP NFT belongs to the protocol forever. Your only return is the vesting schedule. There is no unbond path, even if you change your mind 5 minutes later.

The vesting schedule is keyed to the address that bonded. If that address is gone, the CAH is unrecoverable. Treat the bonder address like any other long-term key.
