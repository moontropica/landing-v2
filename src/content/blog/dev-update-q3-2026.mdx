Another quarter, another update. This one is dense with build. V2's art direction is done, the physics are tuned, three new faces are on the roster, and the treasury has been quietly working. Here is everything that changed in Q3 2026.

## New Characters

V2's roster keeps getting more interesting. Three new characters join the cast this quarter.

**2pac (Musician).** One of the most influential figures in hip-hop history. An iconic rapper whose music and presence defined a generation. In the arena he brings that same larger-than-life energy.

**Bernie Sanders (Politician).** A U.S. senator and two-time presidential candidate, known for decades of advocacy around economic fairness and the working class. Straight-talking, relentless, and never short on conviction.

**Dario Amodei (Anthropic CEO).** The co-founder and CEO of Anthropic, the AI safety company behind Claude. One of the sharpest minds in AI, now stepping into the arena with the same focus he brings to frontier research.

Each one adds a different flavor to the fight. That is the point.

## V2 Art and Physics

The big headline: graphic and model design for V2 is complete. The world is fully modeled and polished, from the low-poly trees and coastline to the details on the ground. What you see in the renders is the real thing, not a mockup.

![Muscle Beach in the V2 world](/assets/blog/v2-muscle-beach.jpg)

![Mystic forest in the V2 world](/assets/blog/v2-mystic-forest.jpg)

Physics got the same treatment. Modifications and optimizations across the board make movement and combat feel tighter and cleaner. The game plays as good as it looks.

## Prototyping

Design work is moving into new territory.

- **1P story mode.** A single player campaign is in prototyping. A real narrative arc to play through, not just arena fights.
- **Dungeon prototyping.** New dungeon concepts are being worked out. Spaces to explore and fight through beyond the open arena.

![Hedge maze zone in the V2 world](/assets/blog/v2-hedge-maze.jpg)

- **Vehicle prototyping.** Vehicle systems are on the bench. New ways to move through the world are coming.

None of this is shipped yet. It is all being prototyped, which is exactly where it should be.

## Moon Tropica Lite (Experimental)

We are also experimenting with Moon Tropica Lite, a 2D retro version of the game. Same world, different frame.

Right now we are analyzing the best way to make the Lite version feel more like an RPG than a straight battle game. The combat loop is already strong, so the question is how much of the wider adventure, progression, and exploration edges its way in. That is the design conversation happening right now.

## Token Economics

The treasury has been moving. Two numbers worth knowing:

- **$35,000 in buybacks.** The team is buying CAH back off the market. That is the team putting its own capital behind the token and pulling supply out of circulation. It is real conviction, shown with real money.
- **$125,000 in liquidity adds.** Through the team and the bonding program, $125K of liquidity has been added to the pool. Permanent, protocol-owned depth.

Combined, that is $160,000 the team moved to work for the ecosystem this quarter. At a fixed, renounced supply of 35M, this kind of activity does not dilute anyone.

## What's Next

V2 keeps building. The art is done, the physics are tight, the roster is growing, and the treasury is compounding. Prototyping on story, dungeons, and vehicles sets up the next stretch.

As always, built by the CAHmunity, for the CAHmunity.
