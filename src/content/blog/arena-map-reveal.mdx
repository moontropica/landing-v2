## The Design Process

When we first sat down to sketch the new arena, we knew it had to feel different. The old map favored long-range engagements, but we wanted something that rewarded aggressive, close-quarters play.


The layout draws inspiration from amphitheaters — a central pit surrounded by elevated platforms. Fighters in the center have sightlines cut by crumbling pillars and overgrown ruins, while snipers on the upper ring must contend with limited cover.

## Verticality Is Everything

The biggest change is vertical flow. We added jump pads, grappling hooks, and destructible bridges that collapse mid-match. If you're standing still, you're already dead.

The map was designed with three core traversal paths:
1. **The Pit** — high risk, high reward. Control the center and you control the power weapon spawns.
2. **The Ring** — safer rotations with long sightlines, but exposed to grenade spam.
3. **The Undercroft** — underground tunnels connecting all four quadrants. Fast rotations, but tight corridors make for deadly ambushes.

## What's Next

This is the first of three maps we're shipping this quarter. The next one is a frozen tundra with dynamic weather. Stay tuned.
