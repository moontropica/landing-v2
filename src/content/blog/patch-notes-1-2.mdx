## Weapon Tuning

- **Frostbite SMG**: Damage falloff increased from 15m to 18m. Close-range TTK unchanged.
- **Javelin DMR**: Headshot multiplier reduced from 2.5x to 2.2x. Bodyshot damage increased by 5%.
- **Ember Shotgun**: Pellet spread tightened by 10%. One-shots should feel more consistent.
- **Phase Boots**: Cooldown increased from 8s to 10s. Still strong, but you can't spam it endlessly anymore.

## Matchmaking

We rolled out skill-based matchmaking in 5 additional regions. Queue times are down 30% on average, but we're monitoring closely for outliers at the top end of the ladder.

If you're seeing unusually long queues in diamond or above, please report it — we're actively tuning the matchmaker thresholds.

## Bug Fixes

- Fixed an exploit where players could clip through the waterfall on Jungle Temple
- Resolved a server crash caused by simultaneous ultimate activations in 12-player lobbies
- The "Unstoppable" medal now tracks correctly
- Fixed a visual glitch where the Ember Warden's cape would stretch across the entire map (as hilarious as it was)
