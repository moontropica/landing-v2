## Clip of the Month

User **Valkyrie_X** pulled off what might be the cleanest 1v4 clutch we've ever seen. Three seconds on the clock, no shields, and a gravity hammer. Watch the clip — it's absolute cinema.

The play starts with Valkyrie low on health, pinned behind a broken pillar on the Arena map. Instead of peeking, they baited all four opponents into chasing, then launched off a jump pad and delivered a perfect gravity hammer slam from above. All four eliminated in a single swing.
